# ADR-NNN — Decision title

**Status:** Proposed

## Context

What forces, constraints, and evidence make a decision necessary?

## Options considered

Include the simplest credible option.

## Decision

State the choice and scope precisely.

## Consequences

Record benefits, costs, risks, operational burden, and effects on future changes.

## Validation and reversal

What evidence will validate the decision? Which signal would cause reconsideration, and how reversible is it?
